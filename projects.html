AFEW-VA database for Valence and Arousal estimation in-the-wild

The AFEW-VA dataset provides per-frame valence and arousal labels, together with 68 facial landmarks, for 600 challenging video clips drawn from feature films. The clips complement the discrete emotion annotations available in the AFEW database.
